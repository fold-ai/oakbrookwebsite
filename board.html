Our Village police liaison

Through the Oak Brook Police Department’s HALO program, Forest Glen has a dedicated liaison officer for neighborhood safety questions and concerns: Officer Franczak — (630) 706-4069 · afranczak@oak-brook.org. In an emergency, always call 911.

Interested in serving?

The association is run by neighbors, for neighbors. Under our by-laws the board has seven directors, elected by the homeowners at the Annual Meeting in June and serving as volunteers. If you would like to stand for a seat or help with a committee, just speak with any current director — new energy is always welcome.

Forest Glen
