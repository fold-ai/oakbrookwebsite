Where do we meet?

Meeting locations can vary from month to month. To find out where (and at what time) an upcoming board meeting will be held, please contact one of the members of the Board of Directors — any of us will be happy to tell you.

Community events

At Forest Glen, we enjoy throwing yearly events for friends and neighbors to catch up and have some fun. From food and drinks and a relaxing environment for adults to take a break, to activities and games for kids to enjoy, our events really bring together all of what Forest Glen stands for. Events like the community picnic and the Halloween Children’s Haunted Walk are posted on this calendar alongside board meetings — click on an event within the calendar to see more information.

Never miss a meeting

Each upcoming meeting has an “Add to calendar” button — tap it to save the date straight to the calendar on your phone or computer. Once a location is confirmed with a board member, you can note it right on that saved entry.
