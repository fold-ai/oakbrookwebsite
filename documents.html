Documents & Helpful Links

Here you can find more in-depth information about the Forest Glen Homeowners’ Association — like our by-laws, describing what Forest Glen is about.

Governing Documents

The papers behind the neighborhood

Every lot owner in Forest Glen is automatically a member of the association, and these two documents set out how it all works. They open right in your browser — and you can download a copy for your records.

By-Laws

PDF · 7 pages · as amended June 1, 2004

The rules of the Forest Glen Community Homeowners’ Association itself: membership and voting rights, the annual meeting, the seven-member Board of Directors and its officers, committees, records, and how the by-laws can be amended.

View PDF Download

Declaration of Easements, Covenants & Restrictions

PDF · 14 pages · recorded July 15, 1976 (DuPage County, Doc. R76-46769), with later amendments

The covenants that run with every lot in the subdivision: land use and building standards, architectural controls, easements, assessments, and the powers of the association and the Village.

View PDF Download

Have a question about the documents?

If anything in the by-laws or the declaration is unclear — mailbox guidelines, assessments, architectural approvals — any member of the Board of Directors will be glad to point you in the right direction.
